The original field recording was made in Rio Acima, Minas Gerais, Brazil, and features Mel howling in response to distant dogs. The source recording is 1:31.989, 96 kHz / 24-bit / stereo. This repository contains a 5.5-second mono MP3 excerpt from 00:39.5 to 00:45.0, faded at the edges and reduced in bitrate for use as a rare nighttime companion-dog howl.
